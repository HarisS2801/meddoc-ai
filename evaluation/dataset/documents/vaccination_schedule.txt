SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - ADULT IMMUNIZATION SCHEDULE
Educational reference material. Contains general, widely published
immunization guidance reformulated for synthetic training data.

INFLUENZA
- Annual influenza vaccination is recommended for all adults each year.

TDAP
- A single Tdap booster is recommended every 10 years for all adults.
- Pregnant individuals should receive Tdap during each pregnancy.

ZOSTER (SHINGRIX)
- Recommended for all adults aged 50 years and older.
- Two doses given 2 to 6 months apart.
- Adults who received the older live zoster vaccine should be
  revaccinated with Shingrix.

PNEUMOCOCCAL
- Recommended for all adults aged 65 years and older.
- Also recommended for adults with certain chronic conditions regardless
  of age.

COVID-19
- Updated COVID-19 vaccine doses are recommended as indicated for age and
  risk group.

HEPATITIS B
- Recommended for adults with diabetes aged 19 through 59 years.

CONSULT
- Ask the care team or a pharmacist if you have questions about which
  vaccines apply to you.
